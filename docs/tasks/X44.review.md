## Flake fixes

(a) Acceptable, but not as proposed. "Poll until the box stops moving" is also true of a popup that has not
started to move, which is the recorded failure (field bottom 1972 px, keyboard 1541 px). Wait for named
conditions inside the existing 10 s deadline: the keyboard's edge equal on two reads (the previous driver's
condition), the field's box different from its pre-focus box (the previous driver asserted "the popover moves
clear"), then equal on two reads. Then the assertions unchanged; at the deadline fall through to them so the
failure prints the same numbers. Never poll the placement assertion: a popup passing through a valid spot passes.

(b) Re-reading the ref and tapping again is a retry and names no condition. It can be deterministic:
`screen.tapAt(await centreOf(day))`, the pattern at `stepper.e2e.ts:35` and `segmented-control.e2e.ts:32`, or
`day.tap({ position })`, which the driver documents as skipping its actionability checks.
`toHaveText(target.iso)` still proves the right cell took the finger.

## Phase-2 duration

Nearly all of it is dump latency. Every read is `uiautomator dump` plus `cat`, two adb round trips and about
2 s (`android-tree-checks.mjs:50`): about 130 reads in the check bodies, 22 press loops that dump on every poll,
three to six dumps of navigation before each check, and at least three per sweep case. Fixed sleeps are small.
"One dump per check" is not possible as stated: a check alternates input and reads, and a tree is reusable only
between two inputs. The small change is a snapshot cached in `makeContext` and dropped on every input, sleep and
poll (about 30 lines, check bodies untouched); it shares adjacent reads and saves a few minutes, not fourteen.
Polls must never be served from it, or the negative assertions can no longer fail. Reaching 30 minutes means
restructuring the check bodies: a rewrite of moved code, and twelve red proofs again.
